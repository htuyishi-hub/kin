How it works

A mutual friend, not an algorithm.

Kin manufactures the feeling of a trusted introduction. Every step is designed to remove hesitation and protect your dignity.

1. Register with your phone

Your Rwandan number is your identity. No password. A six-digit OTP arrives in seconds via MTN or Airtel.

2. Get vouched for

Select 2–3 people from your faculty, hostel, or church who know you personally. Their confirmation activates your profile.

3. Browse with trust context

Every profile shows shared context first — same hostel, same fellowship, mutual connections — before you see a photo.

4. The system breaks the ice

When you match, Kin randomly assigns one person to ask the first question. No awkward silence. The app takes the risk.

5. A 15-minute hello

The app suggests a local meetup spot and frames it as a quick, no-pressure hello. No trapped long dinner. Just a start.

6. Kin steps away

After a second real meetup, Kin asks if you want to move your connection to real life. The app exits when its job is done.

Why Kin is different

Built from the voice of your community — not Silicon Valley.

Global apps were designed in English for Western assumptions. Kin was designed from a real conversation with a woman in Masaka who asked: "If I am here, how will a person from Gicumbi know I exist?"

  • ✦ Kinyarwanda first. Every prompt, every system message, every introduction card speaks in your language.
  • ✦ MTN MoMo and Airtel only. No bank card. No international gateway. No one is locked out by payment method.
  • ✦ Purpose before attraction. Life vision and values are shared before physical appearance becomes the focus.
  • ✦ Kin earns money when users succeed. Not when they stay lonely and scrolling.

Terms of Service & Privacy

1. Who we are

Kin is a trusted introduction network for serious relationships, built for users in Rwanda. When we say "Kin", "we", "us", or "our", we mean the Kin platform and its operators. By creating an account, you agree to these Terms of Service and our Privacy Policy.

Plain language: Kin is not a dating app in the Western sense. It is a community-verified introduction platform. These terms reflect that purpose.

2. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to use Kin. By registering, you confirm that:

  • You are 18 years of age or older.
  • You have a valid Rwandan mobile phone number registered in your real name.
  • You are providing honest, accurate information about yourself.
  • You are using Kin for genuine relationship purposes — not for commercial solicitation, scamming, or any form of exploitation.

Kin reserves the right to verify your age and identity at any time and to permanently remove any account found to be in violation of these requirements.

3. Your data and privacy

We collect only what we need to run the platform:

  • Phone number — your primary identifier, stored securely and never shared.
  • Profile information — name, age, gender, campus or location, and your answers to profile prompts.
  • Photos — stored in a secure CDN. Never shown to any third party.
  • Messages — stored only as long as a conversation is active. We do not read your messages except in the case of a formal safety report.
We never sell your data. We never share your data with advertisers. We never give your information to any third party for any commercial purpose. This is not a policy we can quietly change — it is built into the architecture of how the product works.

Your student ID photo, if submitted for verification, is seen only by the Kin moderation team and is permanently deleted within 7 days of successful verification. It is never shown to other users.

Your National ID number, if submitted for adult verification, is processed by a licensed identity verification partner and is never stored on Kin's servers. Only a boolean verification flag is retained.

4. Location and Bluetooth

Kin uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and GPS geofencing optionally to verify that you attended a meetup with your match, enabling the bravery reward system.

  • These features are strictly opt-in. You are shown a plain-language consent screen in Kinyarwanda before any location or Bluetooth service activates.
  • We never track your real-time live location.
  • We never store your location history.
  • Location and Bluetooth services activate only during a 40-minute window around a scheduled meetup, and shut down immediately after the verification event is recorded.
  • Declining location consent does not block access to any core feature of Kin. You simply will not receive the automated meetup reward.

5. Payments

All payments are processed through MTN Mobile Money (MoMo) or Airtel Money. Kin does not store any payment card details. All transactions are in Rwandan Francs (RWF).

  • Serious Connection Tokens (1,000 RWF for 3 tokens) — consumable. Grants introduction requests in the serious match pool.
  • Crossing Fee (2,500 RWF, one-time) — grants a student access to the Life Lounge for marriage-intent matching.
  • Profile Boost (500 RWF / 24 hours) — increases profile visibility for one day.
  • Second Chance (100 RWF) — recovers an accidentally skipped profile.
  • Alumni Premium (2,000 RWF / month) — for graduates maintaining access after leaving campus.
Refund policy: Payments are non-refundable except in the case of a verified technical error on Kin's side (for example, payment processed but feature not delivered). Contact support@kin.rw within 7 days of such an error. Tokens already used are not refundable.

Kin may update pricing with at least 14 days notice communicated inside the app. Tokens already purchased are not affected by price changes.

6. Community standards

Kin is a space for serious people meeting for serious reasons. The following are not permitted and will result in immediate and permanent removal:

  • Using Kin to seek financial arrangements or any form of transactional relationship.
  • Harassment, threats, or disrespectful communication of any kind.
  • Misrepresenting your identity, age, or intentions.
  • Contacting a match outside the platform before establishing trust inside it.
  • Any attempt to exploit, deceive, or harm another user.
  • Creating an account if you are under 18 years of age.

When you vouch for someone, you are lending them your reputation within the Kin community. Vouch only for people you genuinely know and can genuinely speak for. If a person you vouched for behaves badly, your own visibility in the platform is reduced.

7. Content you submit

You retain ownership of any content you submit to Kin — photos, profile answers, and messages. By submitting content, you grant Kin a limited license to display that content within the platform for the purpose of operating the service.

With your explicit written consent, Kin may use anonymised success stories (as described in the Exit Prompt flow) for community trust-building. This consent is always separate, always optional, and always revocable. We will never use your story publicly without your prior approval of the specific content and attribution.

You must not submit content that is false, misleading, sexually explicit, violent, or that infringes on any third party's rights.

8. Account deletion and data removal

You can delete your account at any time from Settings > Account > Delete account. On deletion:

  • Your profile is immediately hidden from all other users.
  • All your personal data — profile, photos, messages, vouch records — is permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Nothing is archived, retained, or transferred.
  • Paid tokens that have not been used are forfeited. They are not refundable on voluntary account deletion.
If Kin ever closes: All user data will be permanently deleted within 90 days of any closure announcement. You will receive notice inside the app at least 30 days before. No data will be sold, transferred, or passed to any third party.

9. Liability and disclaimers

Kin facilitates introductions between verified users. We do not guarantee any specific outcome — including successful relationships, meetups, or compatibility between matched users.

Kin is not responsible for the conduct of users outside the platform. We strongly encourage meeting for the first time in a public place, in daylight, and informing a trusted person of your plans. If you ever feel unsafe, leave immediately and report the situation to us at safety@kin.rw.

To the extent permitted by Rwandan law, Kin's liability to any individual user is limited to the total amount paid by that user to Kin in the 30 days preceding any claim.
